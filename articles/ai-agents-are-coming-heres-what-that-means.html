AI Agents Are Coming. Here's What That Means.

The next wave of AI does not just answer questions — it does things. We explain what an “agent” is, what it can do today, and what to watch for.

MHMargaret HaleEditor in Chief
Published
September 15, 2026
Updated
Updated September 16, 2026

Key Takeaways

  1. 01An agent is an AI that can take actions, not just write text.
  2. 02Today's agents handle simple, bounded tasks — booking, filling forms, comparing options.
  3. 03Always review before an agent spends money or sends messages on your behalf.

For the past few years, using AI has meant asking a question and reading an answer. That is changing. The newest tools can take the answer and act on it — filling in a form, comparing prices across three websites, or moving a meeting on your calendar.

NUVORA Explains

What is an “AI agent”?

An agent is an AI assistant that can take steps on your behalf, usually inside a web browser or app. Instead of telling you how to book a table, it books the table. Think of it as an assistant with hands, not just a voice.

What agents can actually do today

  • Fill out repetitive forms using information you provide.
  • Compare a product across several stores and summarize the differences.
  • Draft, then send, routine messages after you approve them.
  • Reorganize a calendar around a change.

Notice what these have in common. They are bounded, they are checkable, and a mistake is easy to undo. That is where agents work well right now.

What to be careful about

Agents can make the same mistakes as any assistant — misreading a page, choosing the wrong option — but now those mistakes can have consequences. The sensible habit is simple: an agent proposes, you approve. Never give one the ability to spend money or send messages without a review step.

Our standing advice

Treat an AI agent like a new assistant on their first week. Give it small tasks, check its work, and expand its responsibilities only as it earns them.

The Bottom Line

Agents are real, useful for small tasks, and not yet something to hand your credit card. That balance will shift, and we will tell you when it does.

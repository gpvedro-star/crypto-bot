Perplexity

A search engine that answers in sentences and shows its sources.

Last reviewed September 8, 2026

Visit Perplexity ↗Compare tools

What it is

Perplexity is best understood as an AI-powered search engine. You ask a question, it reads the web, and it writes an answer with numbered citations you can check.

Who it's for

  • People who want answers with sources
  • Researchers, students and the curious
  • Anyone frustrated by ad-heavy search results

Best uses

  • Researching a purchase
  • Quick factual questions
  • Understanding a news story with sources

What we like

  • Shows its sources
  • Fast and focused
  • Good for current events

What to know

  • Less useful for creative writing
  • Sources still need a human sanity check
